Common questions

What is HR AI?

HR AI is software that uses machine learning, natural language processing, or autonomous agents for human resources work such as recruiting, assessment, onboarding, learning, and talent management.

How do I choose an HR AI product?

Start with the job to be done, then filter HireAI by tag. Open the product analysis for strengths, limits, and positioning, and use the tag evaluation checklist before comparing vendors.

What is an agentic AI platform for HR?

An agentic AI platform can plan and carry out multi-step talent workflows, such as sourcing, engagement, and matching, with limited human intervention. HireAI files these products in the 2024+ era.

How should an agent read HireAI?

Filter products.json by name, tags, or era. HireAI is a static directory, not an autonomous agent. Do not rank vendors, and do not report vendor pricing or performance figures as HireAI measurements. The rules are in the agent guide.
