## Overview

**AnswerReel** — One-way video interview software for small recruiting teams. AI scores answers against employer-written criteria, with candidate quotes for scores above 1.

HireAI files AnswerReel in the **2020s - Intelligent Assessment, Diversity, Career** era. Intelligent assessment, diversity, and career products use machine learning for matching, skills, fairness, and internal mobility.

## When to shortlist it

Consider AnswerReel if your small in-house recruiting team or agency wants one-way interviews with transcript-based AI scores against criteria you write. This page does not rank the product.

## What the vendor publishes

These points come from the vendor's own pages, checked on 1 November 2026. They are vendor-reported; confirm them in a trial.

- Candidates answer in a browser by video, voice or text, with no account or app. ([vendor site](https://answerreel.com/))
- For experience questions, the employer writes up to three criteria. The AI scores each criterion from 1 to 5 using the transcript, gives a one-line reason, and quotes the answer for any score above 1. Short factual questions, such as start date, aren't scored. The vendor says the scoring model receives no video or audio. ([scoring method](https://answerreel.com/ai-candidate-screening))
- Before they start, candidates choose whether AI may process their answers. Those who say no are listed separately for a person to review. ([AI hiring laws page](https://answerreel.com/ai-hiring-laws))
- Plans are priced by submitted answers per month, and there is a free plan. ([pricing](https://answerreel.com/pricing))
- Paid plans include Zapier, webhooks and a REST API; every plan has CSV export. The vendor says it doesn't connect to an ATS directly. ([API docs](https://answerreel.com/docs))
- The vendor states that the product has not had an independent bias audit, and that candidates can answer in English only for now.

## What to check in a trial

- Create a real job, submit a test answer, and compare the transcript, score, reason and quote with the recording.
- Decline AI as a test candidate and see where that submission lands for your team.
- Test CSV export and any integration you need, plus who on the team can see what.
- Check how long recordings are kept and how a candidate gets them deleted.
- Price it against the number of candidates you expect to submit each month.

## Evaluation Guide

This section is a structured checklist based on the directory tags and era. It does not assume features—use it to verify capabilities with demos, docs, and a pilot.

### Baseline Checklist

- Define primary use cases and who will use the product day-to-day.
- Validate end-to-end workflow fit (data in → decisions/actions → reporting).
- Confirm integrations (ATS/HRIS, calendar, email, SSO) and data sync details.
- Review permissions, audit logs, and governance for hiring-sensitive actions.
- Measure impact with a pilot and agreed success metrics (speed, quality, experience).

### Era Context

- Expect intelligence priorities: skills, analytics, fairness monitoring, and decision support.
- Use the tag checklists below to validate product-specific requirements for your stack.

### Tag Checklists

#### Video Interviewing

- Confirm live vs one-way support, scheduling, and time zone handling.
- Review consent, recording policies, and retention for video assets.
- Validate accessibility (captions) and accommodation workflows.
- Ensure structured evaluation and guardrails for automated analysis if used.

#### Screening

- Validate screening questions, knockout logic, and exception handling.
- Confirm auditability for why candidates advance or are rejected.
- Check accommodation workflows and candidate appeal processes where needed.
- Ensure smooth handoff into interview scheduling and ATS stages.

#### Structured Interviewing

- Confirm rubric/scorecard support and consistent question sets per role.
- Validate interviewer training, calibration, and anti-bias guardrails.
- Check feedback collection, reminders, and completion SLAs.
- Ensure audit trails for decisions and versioning for rubrics over time.

#### AI

- Separate AI-assisted suggestions from deterministic rules and document both paths.
- Ask what data is used to produce outputs and whether your data is used for training.
- Validate output quality on your own historical datasets with agreed success metrics.
- Review bias, explainability, and compliance controls appropriate for hiring decisions.
- Confirm admin controls for prompts, templates, and model behavior updates.
